# Project showcase snippets

## Simple and reliable

```markdown
### PROJECT TITLE

**Problem:** What were you trying to solve?

**Approach:** What did you build or investigate?

**Result:** What did you find, demonstrate, or learn?

[Repository](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/PROJECT_NAME)
```

## In-progress research

```markdown
### CURRENT RESEARCH — In progress

I'm investigating QUESTION using METHOD. My next milestone is MILESTONE.

[Working notes or public code](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/PROJECT_NAME)
```

Only link to work you are authorized to share publicly.
