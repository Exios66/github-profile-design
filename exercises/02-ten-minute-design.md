# Exercise 2 — Ten-minute design pass

**Outcome:** Improve hierarchy without adding clutter.

1. Write a one-line headline about your field.
2. Organize the profile into two to four clear sections.
3. Feature one project, or an honestly labeled learning goal.
4. Add one consistent badge or relevant visual.
5. Preview in light and dark mode, and at mobile width.

**Reflection:** What would a new collaborator learn in 30 seconds?
