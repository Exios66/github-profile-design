# Exercise 4 — Peer review

Swap public profile URLs with a partner. Spend two minutes reviewing each profile.

- Can you identify the person's field or interests quickly?
- Is one piece of work or learning clearly explained?
- Do all links work?
- Is anything hard to read or visually distracting?
- Is the content truthful and appropriate for a public audience?

Give **one strength** and **one specific improvement suggestion**. The author chooses what to change.
