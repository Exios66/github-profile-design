# 06 — Quality, accessibility, and maintenance

Before sharing your profile, review:

- **Accuracy:** Your experience, research, and project status are represented truthfully.
- **Accessibility:** Descriptive image alt text, meaningful links, readable contrast, and sensible heading order.
- **Responsive layout:** The profile remains usable on a phone.
- **Privacy:** No tokens, passwords, private email addresses, or unpublished sensitive research.
- **Maintainability:** Avoid unnecessary widgets, dependencies, and stale statistics.
- **Link integrity:** Open each link and check image loading.

Run through the [publication checklist](../CHECKLIST.md), then ask a peer what they learned about your work after 30 seconds of reading.
