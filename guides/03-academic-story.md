# 03 — Tell your academic story

A compelling profile answers three questions:

1. **Who are you?** Your field, program, or professional direction.
2. **What are you exploring?** Research questions, methods, or meaningful interests.
3. **What can visitors inspect?** Repositories, reproducible examples, demos, or documentation.

## Example structure

```markdown
# Hello, I'm YOUR NAME
I'm a graduate student exploring TOPIC.

## Research interests
- QUESTION ONE
- QUESTION TWO

## Featured project
**PROJECT NAME** — Problem, approach, and what I learned.
[Explore the repository](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/PROJECT)
```

If you do not yet have a finished project, write **Currently learning** and explain your next step. Never invent achievements, publications, or technical proficiency.

**Practice:** Rewrite one vague claim (e.g., “I love AI”) as a concrete research interest or project objective.
